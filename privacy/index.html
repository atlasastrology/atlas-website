This Privacy Policy explains how Atlas Astrology LLC, a California limited liability company ("Atlas," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Atlas mobile application and related website and services (together, the "Service").

Please read this Policy together with our Terms of Service. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Plain-language summary (not a substitute for the full Policy): Atlas is an astrology app for entertainment and self-reflection. To build your charts we collect your birth date, birth time, and birth place, plus basic account details. We use your own servers and a small number of trusted service providers to run the app. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral ("targeted") advertising. You can delete your account and data at any time from within the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information we process through the Atlas iOS app and the Atlas website. It does not apply to third-party products or services we do not control, including Apple's App Store, Sign in with Apple, and App Store billing, which are governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Account and profile — Name, email address, phone number, gender, and profile photo.
  • Birth data (sensitive) — Your date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth (city, state, country, precise latitude/longitude, time zone, and elevation).
  • Charts you create for other people — Names, birth dates, birth times, birth locations, and any photos you choose to add for friends, family, or others whose charts you build (see Section 3.4).
  • Journal entries — Ratings (0–5) and the location/date associated with an entry. Your free-text journal reflections are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Purchases — Records of in-app purchases and subscriptions, including Apple transaction identifiers. We never receive or store your full payment-card number — Apple processes all payments.
  • Communications — Feature requests, support messages, and other content you send us.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and push — Device type, device identifier, and push-notification token (used to deliver Apple Push Notifications).
  • Product analytics — In-app events (e.g., screens viewed, features used) recorded in our own first-party database to understand how the Service is used and to improve it. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs in the iOS app.
  • Location — We use the birth and life-event locations you enter. The app does not continuously track your real-time GPS location in the background.
  • Website data — If you use our website, we and our hosting/analytics providers may collect standard log data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed). See Section 8 (Cookies).

3.3 Sensitive personal information

Your birth date, birth time, precise birth location, and any profile photographs may be considered sensitive or special-category data under laws such as the GDPR and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). We collect this information only to provide the core astrology features you request (calculating and displaying your charts, transits, astrocartography, and related insights). Where the law requires consent to process sensitive data, you provide that consent by entering the data and using these features, and you may withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account.

3.4 Information about other people

If you create a chart for another person, you are providing us with their personal (and possibly sensitive) information. You represent and warrant that you have the right and any necessary permission to provide that information, and you agree to inform those individuals about how their data is used as described in this Policy. If you are not authorized to share someone's birth data, please do not enter it.
