Common questions

How is my chart calculated?
Atlas builds every chart on your device using the Swiss Ephemeris. Your birth details are used only to create your charts and readings.

How do I delete my account and data?
Open Atlas, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, charts, and associated data. You can also email us and we will help.

I bought a chart unlock but do not see it.
Make sure you are signed into Atlas with the same account you used, then reopen the app so it can sync your unlocks. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.

Do you offer refunds?
Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page. If something went wrong on our end, email us and we will help.
